About J&F Building

A principal-led firm for considered residential building — based in Buckhurst Hill, working across Essex and London.

Provenance

A Standard We Keep Personally

J&F Building was founded by Jack and Finley on a simple conviction: the quality of a build is decided by who actually does the work. We remain on the tools, on site, on every commission.

Our work spans large extensions, full refurbishments and period properties — projects where the detail matters and the finish is expected to last.

Based in Buckhurst Hill, we work across Essex and London, frequently alongside architects and interior designers. References from past clients are available on request.

How We Work

The Standards We Keep

Craft

Every line, junction and finish is checked by the people who made it. Nothing leaves our hands that we wouldn't stand behind in ten years' time.

Certainty

A clear programme, transparent costing, and honest conversations when decisions are needed. No surprises at the final account.

Principal-Led

You deal with Jack and Finley directly, from first visit to final walkthrough. No intermediaries, no hand-offs.

Discretion

We're accustomed to working in occupied homes and to respecting our clients' privacy. NDAs are available on request, as are references.
